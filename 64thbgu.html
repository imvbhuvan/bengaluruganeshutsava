Experience the Magnificent Grand Procession

Witness the most spectacular event of the 63rd Bengaluru Ganesha Utsava - a grand procession that will traverse through the heart of Basavanagudi starting from BMS College of Engineering to Ramakrishna Aashram Circle through Gandhi Bazar Circle and DVG Road.
