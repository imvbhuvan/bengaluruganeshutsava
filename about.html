About Bengaluru Ganesh Utsava

Celebrating Culture, Unity, and Tradition Since 1962

Bengaluru Ganesh Utsava (BGU) is more than a festival — it is a living celebration of India's vibrant cultural spirit. With a glorious legacy spanning over 60 years, BGU is proudly organised by Sri Vidyaranya Yuvaka Sangha, a not-for-profit organisation committed to the preservation and promotion of Indian heritage.

Rooted in devotion and elevated by artistic brilliance, the Utsava brings together music, dance, tradition, and community in a divine confluence. What began as a humble neighbourhood celebration has today evolved into one of South India's most iconic cultural festivals, drawing artists, performers, and devotees from across the country.

BGU stands as a beacon of cultural unity, seamlessly blending the timeless charm of Indian traditions with contemporary expressions of art and performance.

Celebrating India's Cultural Heritage

Our Mission

To preserve and promote India's rich cultural heritage by fostering diversity, encouraging artistic excellence, engaging communities, and nurturing the next generation through educational and cultural initiatives.

Our Vision

To be a leading cultural movement that bridges tradition and modernity, inspiring future generations to embrace, celebrate, and carry forward India's timeless legacy.


Our Core Values

Cultural Heritage

Upholding and showcasing the richness of Indian traditions, classical arts, and folk forms.

Community

Strengthening bonds and building an inclusive platform through shared cultural experiences.

Excellence

Curating and delivering events of the highest quality in spirit, execution, and impact.

Unity

Celebrating the diverse threads of Indian culture to foster harmony and collective pride.

Whether it is the divine energy of the Ganesha idol, the grandeur of the performances, or the spirit of collective celebration, Bengaluru Ganesh Utsava continues to be a timeless tribute to India's cultural soul — rooted in devotion, enriched by art, and powered by community.
